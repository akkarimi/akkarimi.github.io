About

{% include molecule.html %}

I study AI and language models and their applications — from social media analysis to health and medical data, and from data augmentation to model robustness under adversarial perturbations. My goal is to find ways to improve AI models and their benefits to society.

Currently I am a postdoctoral researcher at Saarland University, working on chemical language models and trustworthy AI for drug discovery at the Spoken Language Systems (LSV) Lab led by Prof. Dietrich Klakow.

I completed my PhD at the University of Parma under Prof. Andrea Prati at IMP Lab, where I developed adversarial learning and data augmentation techniques for more robust language models.
