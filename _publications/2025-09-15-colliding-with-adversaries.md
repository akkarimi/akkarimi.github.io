The Colliding with Adversaries challenge on robust learning in High Energy Physics was held at ECML PKDD 2025. It consisted of two tasks: generating adversarial examples to attack a deep learning model, and developing a model that is robust to unseen adversarial attacks while maintaining strong performance on clean data. Both tasks used a tabular dataset derived from two simulated CMS collision processes at the LHC, with 87 continuous features per sample, framed as binary classification between jets from top-quark pairs (TTJets) and W-boson jets. More details are available on the <a href="https://collidingadversaries.github.io/">challenge website</a>.
